# Project State

- Purpose: a generic NVDA add-on that reads the `/` and `@` suggestion lists in AI apps (ChatGPT, Claude, OpenCode, WhatsApp). Evidence: [../claude/popup_survey.md](../claude/popup_survey.md). Add-on metadata lives in `buildVars.py`.
- Structure: `globalPlugins/slashMenus/__init__.py` (NVDA layer: locating, session, dynamic key binding) and `detect.py` (pure rules with no NVDA import, tested by `run_tests.py`, 10/10).
- Locating the list, in order: focus on a nameless menu (only within 3 s of a trigger) -> a cached container -> a buffer query on `xml-roles` listbox/menu beside the composer -> a buffer query on `attribute_current` (ChatGPT; its container is not cached, because ChatGPT splits the list into sections) -> a bounded row scan (button/menuitem/listitem, 150 rows in each direction from the composer, attempts 2, 6 and 9 only).
- "Beside the composer": horizontal overlap, and the row lies entirely above or below the composer within 400 px. This excludes the composer's own buttons, WhatsApp's chat list and ChatGPT's sidebar link.
- Highlighted row, in order: SELECTED -> isCurrent -> the only differing class (3+ rows) -> background (a normal colour learned by majority, or from two rows assuming the expected one) -> counting arrow presses.
- Session: arrows, Backspace, Escape, Enter and Tab are bound only after a list is confirmed; unbound on close, on focus leaving the composer, or after 30 s. The key is sent before any scan.
- Log: `slashMenus open/no list/closed` lines with the method and time, never item names.
- First test round (2026-10-10): Claude and WhatsApp are good by the user's account. ChatGPT `@` failed and `/` was slow on each press, OpenCode opened after 3.6 s, and there was one false positive in Claude (a scanned group with no marker). Fixes: a direct `aria-current` row checked first on every press (index -1, compared by its label), rejecting `row scan/counted`, remembering the marker that worked (`prefer`), a scan of 80, and the word-start condition (`at_word_start`). Second round: ChatGPT `/` opened in 187 ms, but NVDA froze 6 times and searches took 5-8 s, most likely from stacked search chains (every character started a new one). Fix: a generation counter (only the newest chain and move poll run), at most 2 row scans per chain (attempts 3 and 8), each capped at 250 ms, and a timing line per step (`current/list/scan/rows`, `far_current`) on every open, no-list and move. Third round: Claude `/` is good (open 125 ms, about 150 ms per press, no freeze). OpenCode failed (two scans stopped at the 250 ms cap), and ChatGPT and WhatsApp logged no line at all, most likely because the word-start condition rejected the trigger. Fix: the condition was removed, one scan per chain (attempt 3) capped at 600 ms, and a `trigger` line records every `/` or `@` with `composer=` on the message box check. Fourth round (04:30): ChatGPT `/` (open 187-234 ms, 140-233 ms per press) and `@` (407 ms) work through `aria-current`; WhatsApp `@` works (open 937 ms with one 202 ms scan, 77 ms per press through the cached list and background colour). No freeze or error. OpenCode and Claude were not tried in this round. Short round (04:32): Claude `/` opens in 155 ms, but two presses took about 1 s (a stale saved list, unchanged for 9 polls). OpenCode `/` opens in 1640 ms (one 937 ms scan); class costs 300-400 ms per press and background 62-77 ms. Fix: markers ordered selected, current, background, class, and `STALE_POLLS = 3` drops the saved list after three polls without change. Round (04:34): Claude `/` is fast (about 150 ms per press). OpenCode works (open 1.5 s, 300-450 ms per press in the long list, about 70 ms in the short one). Claude `@` was not found: its listbox overlaps the composer, and `beside` requires it to sit fully above or below. And after Claude's `/` menu closed, the arrows in the composer read unrelated buttons through a row scan inside the move poll. Fix: `detect.near` (overlap allowed) for lists with a role, no row scan in move polls, and a saved menu that has lost focus counts as closed. Confirmed (04:37): Claude `@` through `list role/selected` (390 ms) and Claude `/` (281 ms, 125-155 ms per press, the session ends when the menu closes).
